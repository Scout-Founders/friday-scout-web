Methodology

The Scout Score stored on an observation is the captured 0–100 result. The unclamped score is the inspected formula total before that cap. A total above 100 is clamped to 100, so one captured 100 can be compatible with more than one raw total.

Evidence-determined means the components, including a Specter penalty when one was required, were fixed by the captured narrative or by an invariant of the formula. Mathematical compatibility means a missing penalty was the only value in the inspected penalty domain that reproduced the captured score. That is not an independent confirmation, and neither class is a measure of later return.

An observation is ambiguous when more than one allowed penalty is compatible with the captured score. Those candidates stay listed. No candidate is stored as the reconstructed score, and a null total is not zero.

The methodology id is the inspected local snapshot. Deployment of that snapshot was not verified, so agreement with the formula is not a production revision identity.
